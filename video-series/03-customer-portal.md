# Episode 3 — The Customer Portal

**Duration:** ~90s · **Demo account:** `client@salisauto.com` / `client123` · **Portal:** `/portal/dashboard`

## Goal
Show the platform from the customer's side: booking an appointment, tracking a live service, approving a quotation, and browsing the parts store. **The booking made here is the job we follow for the rest of the series.**

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | Title card. [OVERLAY: "Episode 3 — The Customer Portal"] | This is SALIS AUTO from your customer's point of view — and it's where our story starts. |
| 0:06 | Customer portal dashboard after login. Pan the nav: Dashboard, Appointments, My Vehicles, Find Garage, Parts Store, Track Service, Quotations, Invoices. | Logged in as a customer, you get a personal dashboard: your vehicles, upcoming appointments, invoices, and live service tracking — all in one place. |
| 0:18 | Open **My Vehicles** → click a seeded vehicle → show service history. | Every vehicle has a full digital history, so customers always know what's been done and when. |
| 0:28 | Open **Find Garage** → search → pick a garage. | Need a service? Find Garage locates a workshop in the network... |
| 0:36 | Open **Appointments** → New Appointment → pick the vehicle, a service type, date/time → confirm. [ZOOM] on the confirmation toast. | ...and booking takes seconds: choose your vehicle, the service, and a time slot. Confirm — and the garage sees it instantly. Remember this booking; we'll follow it through the whole series. |
| 0:52 | Open **Track Service** on a seeded in-progress job. Point at status steps updating. | Once work begins, Track Service shows live progress — checked in, diagnosed, in repair, ready for pickup — updated in real time over WebSockets. |
| 1:04 | Open **Quotations** → view a pending quote → click Approve. | If the garage finds extra work, they send a quotation. The customer approves or declines right here — no phone tag, full transparency. |
| 1:14 | Open **Parts Store**, scroll products briefly. Then **Invoices** list. | There's even a parts store for accessories, and every invoice lives in the portal, ready to download. |
| 1:22 | End card. [OVERLAY: "Next: the Service Advisor picks up this booking"] | That booking we just made? In the next episode, we switch sides and handle it as the service advisor. |
