# Episode 7 — The Finance Portal & Saudi Compliance

**Duration:** ~90s · **Demo account:** `finance@salisauto.com` / `finance123` · **Portal:** `/finance-portal`

## Goal
Show the money side: the invoice generated from our job, payment recording, and the built-in Saudi compliance — 15% VAT, ZATCA QR e-invoicing, Hijri dates.
