# Episode 6 — The Store Keeper Portal

**Duration:** ~75s · **Demo account:** `storekeeper@salisauto.com` / `storekeeper123` · **Portal:** `/store-keeper`

## Goal
Show inventory management: issuing parts to our job, stock levels updating, low-stock alerts, barcode scanning, and AI-driven automated reordering.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | Title card. [OVERLAY: "Episode 6 — The Store Keeper"] | Every repair runs on parts — and parts are money sitting on shelves. The store keeper portal keeps both under control. |
| 0:08 | Log in as `storekeeper@salisauto.com`. Show Stock Overview dashboard. | The stock overview shows every part in the store: quantities, locations, and values, searchable in seconds. |
| 0:18 | Open **Issue Parts** → select our job card → pick the parts → confirm. [ZOOM] on stock count decreasing. | Here's our job from the last episode. Issuing parts against it takes three clicks — and watch the stock count: it updates the moment we confirm, and the parts' cost lands on the job automatically. |
| 0:34 | Open **Low Stock Alerts** → show flagged items. | When stock runs low, the platform flags it before it becomes a problem... |
| 0:42 | Open **Automated Reordering** (`/automated-reordering`) → show predicted demand + suggested reorder. | ...and it goes further: predictive demand forecasting suggests what to reorder and when, based on real usage — so fast-moving parts never run out mid-job. |
| 0:56 | Show **Barcode Scanner** page briefly, then **Receive Stock** with a supplier delivery. | Receiving deliveries is just as quick — scan barcodes, match against the purchase order, and shelves are up to date. |
| 1:08 | End card. [OVERLAY: "Next: money — the Finance Portal"] | Parts issued, job complete. Time to get paid — the finance portal is next. |

## Overlays checklist
- "Parts cost flows to the job automatically" (0:26)
- "AI demand forecasting" (0:46)

## Story beat
Salem's shelves supply Saad's job — three clicks to issue the parts, while the store quietly thinks ahead about what to reorder.
