# Episode 10 — Platform Admin: Multi-Tenant Control

**Duration:** ~75s · **Demo account:** `superadmin@salisauto.com` / `superadmin123` · **Portal:** `/platform-admin`

## Goal
Close the series with the top-level view: managing many garages, suppliers, billing, roles, and system health from one screen — then a recap and call to action.
