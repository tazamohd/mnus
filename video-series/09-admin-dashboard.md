# Episode 9 — The Admin Dashboard

**Duration:** ~90s · **Demo account:** `admin@salisauto.com` / `admin123` · **Portal:** `/dashboard`

## Goal
Show the garage from the owner/manager's seat: the full sidebar with 18 workflow groups, the workshop calendar, the live service-bay dashboard, loyalty program, and analytics.
