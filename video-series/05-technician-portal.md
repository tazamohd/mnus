# Episode 5 — The Technician Portal

**Duration:** ~60s · **Demo account:** `tech@salisauto.com` / `tech123` · **Portal:** `/technician-portal`

## Goal
Show job execution from the workshop floor: the assigned job appears, time is tracked, progress updates flow back to the advisor and the customer's live tracking.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | Title card. [OVERLAY: "Episode 5 — The Technician"] | Out on the workshop floor, technicians don't need dashboards full of finance charts — they need their jobs. That's all this portal shows. |
| 0:08 | Log in as `tech@salisauto.com`. Show the technician's job queue. | Log in with the technician demo account, and the job our advisor assigned in the last episode is sitting right in the queue. |
| 0:17 | Open the job card → show service lines, vehicle details, check-in notes. | Opening it shows everything the tech needs: the service lines, the vehicle's history, and the advisor's check-in notes. |
| 0:27 | Click **Start Job** / start the timer. [ZOOM] on the running timer. | Starting the job starts the clock. Time tracking runs per job, feeding labor costs straight into the invoice later — no paper timesheets. |
| 0:38 | Update job status / mark a task complete. Then split-screen or [CUT] to the customer's Track Service view updating. | As the tech progresses the work, every status change ripples outward — the advisor's Active Jobs board updates, and the customer's live tracker moves in real time. |
| 0:50 | End card. [OVERLAY: "Next: parts — the Store Keeper"] | One thing's missing before this job can finish: parts. Next up, the store keeper. |

## Overlays checklist
- "Time tracking → automatic labor billing" (0:30)
- "One update → advisor + customer, live" (0:42)
