# Episode 8 — The Customer Support Portal

**Duration:** ~60s · **Demo account:** `support@salisauto.com` / `support123` · **Portal:** `/support-portal`

## Goal
Show the support workflow: ticket queue, live chat over WebSockets, SLA tracking, and escalations.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | Title card. [OVERLAY: "Episode 8 — Customer Support"] | Great garages aren't just good with cars — they're good with people. The support portal is where that happens. |
| 0:08 | Log in as `support@salisauto.com`. Show ticket dashboard. | The support demo account opens on the ticket queue: every customer question, sorted by priority and status. |
| 0:17 | Open a ticket → show customer history side panel. | Opening a ticket shows the customer's full history alongside it — their vehicles, jobs, and invoices — so agents never ask a customer to repeat themselves. |
| 0:28 | Open **Live Chat** → show an active chat conversation. | Live Chat runs in real time over the platform's WebSocket connection — customers chat from their portal, agents answer here. |
| 0:38 | Show **SLA Tracking** page with response-time indicators, then **Escalations**. | SLA tracking keeps response times honest, and anything stuck can be escalated with one click. |
| 0:47 | Show **Knowledge Base** and **Customer Ratings** briefly. | A built-in knowledge base answers the common questions, and customer ratings feed back into service quality. |
| 0:55 | End card. [OVERLAY: "Next: the view from the top — Admin Dashboard"] | That's the front line covered. Next: the view from the manager's chair. |

## Overlays checklist
- "Full customer context on every ticket" (0:20)
- "Real-time chat (WebSockets)" (0:30)

## Story beat
Amal has a question; Sara answers it with Amal's whole history on one screen — no "can you repeat your issue?", ever.
