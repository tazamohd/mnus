# Episode 0 — The Story of SALIS AUTO | قصة سالس أوتو

**Duration:** ~75s · **Demo account:** none (pure motion graphics — no app footage needed) · **Audience:** everyone — this is the emotional hook that opens the series

## Goal
Before showing a single screen, make the viewer *feel* the problem. This episode tells the story behind the platform: a real garage morning, the pain points, the question that sparked the idea, and the promise the rest of the series delivers on.

## Story beat
Meet **Abu Khalid (أبو خالد)**, who runs a garage in Riyadh the way his father did — on paper, whiteboards, WhatsApp, and memory. One chaotic morning too many leads to a simple question: *what if the whole garage lived in one system, in our language, under our rules?* That question became SALIS AUTO. The series then follows one car — **Amal's Camry** — through the answer.

## Production notes
- This episode is **rendered as motion graphics in both languages**: `renders/episode-00-story-en.mp4` and `renders/episode-00-story-ar.mp4` (Arabic version has RTL captions and Arabic overlays).
- Voiceover: English at ~140 wpm; Arabic (MSA, warm documentary tone) at ~120 wpm — the Arabic lines above are timed to fit the same beats.
- The chaos montage works best with slightly overlapping, "messy" motion; the one-system turn at 0:30 should feel like a breath — slow everything down for a beat before the dashboard forms.
