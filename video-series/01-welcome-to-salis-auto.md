# Episode 1 — Welcome to SALIS AUTO

**Duration:** ~60s · **Demo account:** none (marketing/landing shots only) · **Audience:** anyone new to the platform

## Goal
In one minute, viewers understand what SALIS AUTO is, who it's for, and why it exists. This is the hook for the whole series.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | SALIS AUTO logo animates in on deep navy. [OVERLAY: "Episode 1 — Welcome to SALIS AUTO"] | Running a garage means juggling bookings, job cards, parts, invoices, and customers — usually across five different tools. |
| 0:08 | Fast montage: login page → main dashboard → workshop calendar → invoice screen. ~2s each. | SALIS AUTO puts all of it in one platform. It's a complete automotive ERP — built for everything from a single workshop to a multi-country franchise network. |
| 0:20 | Main dashboard, slow pan down the sidebar's 18 workflow groups. | The platform follows your real workflow: customer intake, vehicle check-in, diagnostics, parts, service execution, billing, and analytics — in that order. |
| 0:32 | Split-screen: customer portal on a phone frame next to the advisor desktop view. | Every role gets its own portal. Customers book and track their service online, while your team works in screens designed for their exact job. |
| 0:42 | Invoice with ZATCA QR code. [ZOOM] on the QR and the 15% VAT line. | And for the Saudi market, compliance is built in — fifteen percent VAT, ZATCA e-invoicing with QR codes, Hijri dates, and full Arabic support. |
| 0:52 | End card: logo + [OVERLAY: "Try it free — 9 demo accounts. Next: how to log in."] | Best of all, you can try every part of it free, right now. In the next video, I'll show you the demo accounts. Let's go. |
