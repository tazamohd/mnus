# Episode 1 — Welcome to SALIS AUTO

**Duration:** ~60s · **Demo account:** none (marketing/landing shots only) · **Audience:** anyone new to the platform

## Goal
In one minute, viewers understand what SALIS AUTO is, who it's for, and why it exists. This is the hook for the whole series.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
