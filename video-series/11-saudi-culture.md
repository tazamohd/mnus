# Episode 11 — بالسعودي: منصة تفهمنا | Made for Saudi Arabia

**Duration:** ~90s · **Demo account:** `admin@salisauto.com` / `admin123` (+ invoice & settings screens) · **Audience:** Saudi garage owners and staff

## Goal
A dedicated episode in **Saudi dialect**, speaking directly to the Saudi viewer about culture, language, and regulation — showing that the platform wasn't translated for Saudi Arabia, it was *built* for it. This is the only episode narrated in dialect; everything else uses MSA for Arabic.

## Story beat
Abu Khalid gives the tour himself, in his own voice. No feature list — a conversation: "أنت تعرف شغل الورش عندنا…" ("you know how garage work is here…"). Every cultural point lands on a real screen: Hijri dates on documents, the ZATCA QR his accountant no longer worries about, Arabic that reads right-to-left properly, the calendar that survives Ramadan season.

## Production notes
- **Voice**: a warm, natural Saudi voice (male, 40s, Najdi-neutral) — conversational, like a friend showing you his shop, never a formal announcer. If using TTS, pick a Saudi-dialect voice, not MSA.
- **Dialect note**: lines are written Najdi-leaning but broadly neutral (avoid region-specific slang). «تحچي» may be softened to «تتكلم» for a more neutral read.
- **Cultural care**: keep it authentic, not performative — no stock desert/camel imagery; the culture is in the *details* (dates, seasons, trust, language), which is exactly the episode's thesis.
- Record the app with the Arabic/RTL interface active throughout this episode.
- Feature accuracy: everything shown (RTL Arabic UI, Hijri dual dates, 15% VAT, ZATCA QR, Zakat settings, TRN validation, Arabic SMS via Twilio, drag-and-drop calendar, live tracking) is a documented platform feature — don't script beyond them.
