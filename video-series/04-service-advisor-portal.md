# Episode 4 — The Service Advisor Portal

**Duration:** ~90s · **Demo account:** `serviceadvisor@salisauto.com` / `advisor123` · **Portal:** `/service-advisor`

## Goal
Show the front-desk workflow: the customer's booking arrives, the vehicle is checked in, a job card and estimate are created. This is the heart of daily garage operations.

## Script

| Time | On-screen (what to record) | Narration |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 0:00 | Title card. [OVERLAY: "Episode 4 — The Service Advisor"] | The service advisor is the bridge between the customer and the workshop — and their portal is built around exactly that job. |
| 0:07 | Log in as `serviceadvisor@salisauto.com` (quick, 3s). Land on advisor dashboard. | Log in with the advisor demo account, and you land on a dashboard of today's appointments, active jobs, and pending estimates. |
| 0:16 | Open **Appointments** → point at the booking from Episode 3. [ZOOM] | And there it is — the booking our customer made in the last episode, waiting at the top of the list. |
| 0:24 | Open **Vehicle Check-In** → select the appointment → walk the check-in form (mileage, fuel level, condition notes). | When the car arrives, Vehicle Check-In captures its condition: mileage, fuel level, existing damage — protecting both the garage and the customer. |
| 0:38 | Create **Job Card** from the check-in → add service lines → assign a technician. | From check-in, one click opens a job card. Add the service lines, assign a technician, and the job is officially in the workshop. |
| 0:52 | Open **Estimates** → build an estimate on the job → send. [ZOOM] on the send confirmation. | Next, the estimate. Parts and labor are priced from the catalog, and sending it pushes a quotation straight to the customer's portal — the same approval screen we saw in Episode 3. |
| 1:06 | Open **Customer Comms** → show message thread / SMS options. | All communication is logged here too — messages, status updates, even SMS reminders through Twilio. |
| 1:14 | Open **Active Jobs** → show the new job with status. | Active Jobs tracks everything in progress, so the front desk always has an answer when a customer calls. |
| 1:22 | End card. [OVERLAY: "Next: the Technician executes the job"] | The job card is now assigned. Next episode: the technician's side of the story. |

## Overlays checklist
- "Check-in → Job card → Estimate, in one flow" (0:38)
- "Estimates land in the customer's portal" (0:56)
